## 📌 General Overview

> **Abstract Summary:** Although artificial intelligence models achieve high predictive performance across critical domains such as healthcare, finance, and autonomous systems, their opaque decision-making processes pose fundamental challenges regarding trustworthiness, transparency, and accountability. This repository provides the complete replication dataset for our systematic literature review synthesized from **298 peer-reviewed studies** (screened from 845 records across Scopus, Web of Science, IEEE Xplore, and ACM Digital Library).
